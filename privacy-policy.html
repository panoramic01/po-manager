Privacy Policy

Panoramic Ops is an internal operations tool built for employees of Panoramic Building LLC. It is not available to the public and is not distributed outside the company.

What data this app accesses

Panoramic Ops integrates with QuickBooks Online (read-only) to let authorized company owners view accounting data — such as company information and vendor records — for internal reporting and reconciliation purposes.

How data is used

Data retrieved from QuickBooks Online is displayed within the app to authorized users and is not shared with, sold to, or disclosed to any third party. It is not used for advertising or analytics.

Who can access this data

Access to the QuickBooks integration is restricted to a single authorized company owner account within Panoramic Ops. No other employee role can view or connect QuickBooks data through this app.

Data retention

Authentication tokens are stored securely within the app's backend (Google Apps Script Properties) and can be revoked at any time by disconnecting the integration within the app.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to Panoramic Building LLC.
